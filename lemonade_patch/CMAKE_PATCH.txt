Add this single entry to the existing LEMON_BACKENDS list in the root CMakeLists.txt:

    "cake|cake"

The current Lemonade backend registry codegen will discover:

    src/cpp/include/lemon/backends/cake/cake.h
    src/cpp/include/lemon/backends/cake/cake_server.h
    src/cpp/server/backends/cake/cake_server.cpp

Do not hand-edit generated backend registry files or generated docs.

For a source-tree development build, place the compiled `lemonade-cake-bridge`
where `BackendUtils::get_backend_binary_path()` can resolve it, or temporarily
adjust the Cake backend binary resolution during the spike.

The next packaging step should add a backend_versions.json entry and installer
recipe for `lemonade-cake-bridge`; that is intentionally not guessed here.
